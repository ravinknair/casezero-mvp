❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: How do I find their GitHub username?
A: They need to go to github.com, sign in, click their profile.
   Their username is in the URL: github.com/[USERNAME]

Q: What if they don't have a GitHub account?
A: They can create one free at https://github.com/signup (2 minutes)

Q: Can I use their email to add them?
A: No, GitHub needs usernames. You must ask them for their username.

Q: What permission should I give them?
A: Use "Push" - they can view code, create branches, and push changes.

Q: Can I remove them later?
A: Yes, go to Settings → Collaborators → Click the X next to their name

Q: Will they see my private commits before I push?
A: No, they only see what's pushed to GitHub.
